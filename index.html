Our Ethos

A Clothing Brand for Indians,
By Indians

From the looms of Indore to the modern silhouette — we honour the quiet authority of heritage textiles. Every thread is a conversation between ancestral craft and the woman who wears it today. Narmada Atelier exists so that Indian beauty never needs translation.

01 — Pillar

Tradition

Handwoven kora silk, real gold zari butis, and the patient labour of abbla artisans — craft that cannot be rushed, only revered.

02 — Pillar

Modernity

Architectural drapes, cocktail capes, and clean editorial lines — heritage textiles cut for the tempo of contemporary life.

03 — Pillar

You

The modern Indian muse — bridal, festive, everyday regal. Every piece is finished for the woman who claims her title of beauty.

Questions, Answered

Everything you may wish to know before booking a private viewing or commissioning a drape.

Absolutely. Blouse embroidery, border density, moti kinari finishing, and colour stories can be tailored. Lead times vary with handwork — typically 2–6 weeks for bespoke drapes.

Flagship heritage sarees begin around ₹18,500. Lehengas and indo-western commissions are quote-based after a private consultation, depending on zari, abbla, and silhouette.

Dry clean only with a heritage-textile specialist. Store flat or loosely rolled in the butter-paper packaging provided, away from moisture and direct sunlight, to preserve gold zari and mirror work.

Ready pieces may be exchanged within 3 days if unused and tags intact. Bespoke commissions are final once embroidery begins. We do not currently offer rentals.
